What happens when you chat with Miho

  • Your messages and her replies go to a small LLM on my home server in Hong Kong. They're handled in memory and never written to disk or logs.
  • Your browser keeps the conversation in this tab only (session storage). It's gone when you close the tab, or when you press New.
  • The web server logs your IP address, the time and your browser type. The chat service itself only logs a scrambled code made from your IP, plus numbers about each chat: how many messages, how long they were, and how long the reply took. I use this for rate limits and for spotting abuse. Both logs are deleted after 14 days.
  • Before your first message there's a quick human check. It uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which collects some browser and device signals under Cloudflare's privacy policy. If Turnstile can't load, a self-hosted check (ALTCHA) runs instead and sends nothing to third parties.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no accounts. Nothing is sold or shared.
